        Foundations
        
          
        1. Anatomy of a Good Cold Email
        
          
        Seniors and founders scan emails on their phones in 7 seconds. Here is the exact structure that gets replies:
        
          
          
            
        • Subject Line: 4-7 words, personalized with recipient or college. No spam words.
          • 
            
        • The Hook (1 line): Prove you know who they are. Mention a recent blog, product launch, or podcast.
          • 
            
        • The Proof (1-2 lines): Mention your strongest project with tech stack and live metrics or GitHub link.
          • 
            
        • The Frictionless Ask: Ask for something small: a 10-minute chat, a referral for Job ID X, or feedback on a demo.
          • 
            
        • The Easy Way Out: "No worries if you are busy right now." This reduces pressure and paradoxically increases response rates.
          • 
          
        
        
        

        
        
        
          
        Cultural Guide
        
          
        2. Do's and Don'ts for Indian Students
        
          
        Indian colleges often teach 1980s official letter writing. Modern tech companies hate that.
        
          
        
            
        
              ❌ Don't Say:
              
          
                
        • "Respected Sir / Madam"
          • 
                
        • "Kindly do the needful"
          • 
                
        • "I hope this email finds you in good health"
          • 
                
        • "Please give me a chance, I am hardworking"
          • 
                
        • Attaching a 5MB resume without introduction
          • 
              
        
            
        
            
        
              ✅ Instead Say:
              
          
                
        • "Hi [First Name]" or "Hello Prof. [Last Name]"
          • 
                
        • "Could you please share your thoughts on..."
          • 
                
        • "Hope you're having a productive week"
          • 
                
        • "Here is a project I built solving X: [Link]"
          • 
                
        • Upload resume to Google Drive / LinkedIn with view link
          • 
              
        
            
        
          
        
        
        

        
        
        
          
        Research
        
          
        3. How to Approach Professors
        
          
        Professors at IITs, NITs, and foreign universities get 50 mass emails a day. Here is how to stand out:
        
          
          
            
        • Read at least one recent paper: Read the abstract and conclusion of their 2024-2026 publication. Mention it by name in sentence two.
          • 
            
        • Connect your coursework: Mention courses, GPA (if high), or coding repos relevant to their lab's research focus.
          • 
            
        • Don't ask for a "job": Ask about "potential research assistant or summer research intern openings" in their lab.
          • 
            
        • Keep it academic: Use "Dear Prof. [Last Name]". Avoid informal slang. Attach a clean 1-page academic CV link.
          • 
          
        
        
        

        
        
        
          
        Referrals
        
          
        4. How to Ask for a Referral Without Being Awkward
        
          
        Employees get referral bonuses, but they won't risk their reputation for an unprepared stranger.
        
          
          
            
        • Find the exact Job ID: Never say "refer me for any role". Give them the exact URL and Job ID from their careers portal.
          • 
            
        • Provide a 2-line pre-written summary: Write 2 bullets of why you match the job description so they can copy-paste it directly into their internal HR portal.
          • 
            
        • Target Alumni or 2-3 Year Seniors: They remember the college placement struggle and are 5x more likely to refer you than directors.
          • 
            
        • Never badger: If they don't respond, let it go. Do not message 10 people at the same company on the same day.
          • 
          
        
        
        

        
        
        
          
        Tactics
        
          
        5. When and How to Follow Up
        
          
        Over 50% of cold email replies happen on the first follow-up. But timing is critical.
        
          
          
            
        • The 5-7 Day Rule: Wait at least 5 business days before following up. Never bump an email within 24-48 hours.
          • 
            
        • Add Fresh Value: Don't just write "Just checking in". Say: "Quick update: I just pushed v2 of the project with Redis caching."
          • 
            
        • Keep it under 3 sentences: Acknowledge their busy schedule, restate your quick ask, and thank them.
          • 
            
        • Max 2 follow-ups: If they don't reply after 2 polite attempts, move on. Never guilt-trip someone.
          • 
          
        
        
        

        
        
        
          
        Examples
        
          
        6. Winning Subject Line Examples
        
          
        Real, tested subject lines that consistently achieve 40%+ open rates:
        
          
        
            
        
              Internship:
              Question re: [Company]'s backend stack / CS student from [College]
            
        
            
        
              Referral:
              [College] student / Referral inquiry for [Role] (Job ID [1234])
            
        
            
        
              Professor:
              Prospective Research Intern / Question regarding [Paper Name]
            
        
            
        
              Alumni:
              Quick advice from a fellow [College] junior?
            
        
            
        
              Follow-up:
              Re: [Original Subject] (Quick project update)
